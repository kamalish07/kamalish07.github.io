                How this works

                Each visitor's browser adds 1 to anonymous public counters at countapi.mileshilliard.com (no signup, no cookies, no IP addresses stored). This page reads those counters, so it shows traffic from everyone. A "visit" is one browser tab session; "visitors" are distinct browsers. Bots, local previews and browsers with the switch above turned on are not counted.

                Limits: counters are public, so anyone who knows the key names could read or change them. Days are counted in IST. Regions are estimated from the visitor's time zone.
